Topic Tracks

Technical tracks

Speaker proposals are accepted through September 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM Central Time (CT). Applicants must select the technical track most closely aligned with the proposed presentation and identify the applicable UN Sustainable Development Goal(s). The committee may reassign a proposal to a more appropriate track during program development.

01

Smart City Infrastructure, Public Services & Community Resilience

Physical, digital, and public-service infrastructure that enables smart, resilient, inclusive, and sustainable communities.

Example topics: Smart infrastructure, digital construction, BIM/VDC, project controls, smart buildings, data centers, commissioning, asset management, smart governance, digital services, healthcare, public safety, civic technology, accessibility, ethical AI, WIE, YP, student innovation.

Primary: SDG 9, SDG 11, SDG 16Secondary: SDG 3, SDG 4, SDG 5, SDG 8, SDG 10, SDG 13, SDG 17
02

Sustainable Cities, Climate Resilience & Smart Energy

Solutions that help communities become more sustainable, climate-ready, energy-resilient, and environmentally responsible.

Example topics: Climate resilience, sustainable planning, emergency response, environmental monitoring, urban heat, smart lighting, green infrastructure, smart grids, microgrids, renewable energy, storage, demand response, VPPs, EV charging, grid resilience, data center energy demand.

Primary: SDG 7, SDG 11, SDG 13Secondary: SDG 6, SDG 9, SDG 12
03

Smart Mobility, Transportation & Connected Systems

Transportation systems that improve safety, accessibility, efficiency, connectivity, and sustainability across smart communities.

Example topics: EV infrastructure, connected vehicles, autonomous mobility, traffic optimization, public transit, smart parking, logistics, pedestrian safety, fleet electrification, transportation data platforms, smart corridors.

Primary: SDG 9, SDG 11Secondary: SDG 3, SDG 7, SDG 13
04

Data Spaces, AI, Digital Twins & Smart City Platforms

Data, AI, and digital platforms that support smart-city planning, operations, governance, and decision-making.

Example topics: Urban data spaces, AI, digital twins, predictive analytics, open data, governance, generative AI, infrastructure monitoring, city dashboards, simulation tools, integrated smart-city platforms.

Primary: SDG 9, SDG 11, SDG 17Secondary: SDG 13, SDG 16
05

IoT, Sensors, Connectivity, Cybersecurity & Privacy

Connected technology layers that enable reliable smart-city services while protecting privacy, data, security, and public trust.

Example topics: IoT, sensors, 5G/6G, private networks, cyber-physical systems, cybersecurity, privacy, secure edge computing, hardware security, public safety networks, resilient connectivity.

Primary: SDG 9, SDG 11, SDG 16Secondary: SDG 7, SDG 13
06

Semiconductors, AI Hardware, Edge Intelligence & Data Centers

Hardware and computing foundations behind smart-city technologies, including chips, edge devices, AI infrastructure, and data centers.

Example topics: Semiconductors, GPUs, AI accelerators, SoCs, analog/mixed-signal circuits, low-power edge devices, 5G/6G hardware, hardware reliability, sensor hardware, edge AI, AI infrastructure, data centers.

Primary: SDG 7, SDG 9, SDG 11Secondary: SDG 8, SDG 12, SDG 13
