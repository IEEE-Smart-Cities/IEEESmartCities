Call for Speaker Proposals

Share your work at IEEE Smart Cities Dallas

This is a speaker-only event this cycle — we are not running a paper submission or peer-review process. We invite industry professionals, researchers, public-sector leaders, students, WIE, YP, and community stakeholders to propose a session.

Proposal types

  • Technical talk
  • Industry case study
  • Panel discussion
  • Workshop or tutorial
  • Demonstration or showcase
  • Student lightning talk
  • Young Professional or WIE session

Before you submit

  • Review the topic tracks and choose the closest fit.
  • Identify at least one UN SDG your session connects to, and be ready to briefly explain the connection.
  • Have a short bio and abstract ready (see field lengths below).
  • A headshot or LinkedIn link is optional but appreciated.
UN SDG alignment. Every proposal is asked to identify at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal and briefly explain the connection between the goal and the proposed session.
How it works

Simple review workflow

  1. Submit your proposal using the form below.
  2. Your response is recorded by the Technical Program committee for review.
  3. A reviewer or committee owner is assigned and scores the proposal against the review rubric.
  4. The committee decides: accept, panel, workshop, revise, waitlist, or decline.
  5. Accepted speakers receive a confirmation email and are added to the Speakers and Agenda pages.
