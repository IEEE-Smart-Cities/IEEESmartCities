About the Event

Building smart, resilient, and connected communities

Smart cities require cross-disciplinary work — across infrastructure, chips, AI, energy, mobility, governance, cybersecurity, and community impact. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas is a regional technical event being developed to bring these communities together in Plano, TX.

Speaker-focused this cycle

This year's event is planned as a speaker-only program — we are not running a paper submission or peer-review process. The planned program includes invited talks, speaker proposals, panels, case studies, workshops, demonstrations, and dedicated student, Young Professional, and WIE sessions.

Who it's for

Industry professionals, researchers, public-sector and government leaders, academia, students, Women in Engineering (WIE), Young Professionals (YP), and community stakeholders working on any layer of the smart-city stack — from semiconductors to public services.

UN Sustainable Development Goals. Speakers will be encouraged to show how their presentation supports at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG). This helps connect technical work to broader community and global impact.
A note on planning status. This site describes an event that is still being developed. Tracks, agenda, sponsors, venue, and registration details are proposed and may change as the committee finalizes plans — nothing below should be read as final.
