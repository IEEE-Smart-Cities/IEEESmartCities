Sponsorship

Support IEEE Smart Cities Dallas

Sponsorship supports a regional IEEE Smart Cities technical event in Plano, TX — connecting semiconductors, AI, data centers, infrastructure, energy, mobility, and public services. Sponsorship packages and benefits are still being finalized by the committee.

Possible sponsor interests

Let us know which areas interest you — this helps the committee shape sponsorship packages.

  • Event support — general support for the overall event and program.
  • Student / WIE / YP support — sponsoring student, Women in Engineering, and Young Professional participation.
  • Session support — sponsoring a specific track, talk, or panel.
  • Networking support — sponsoring breaks, lunch, or a networking reception.
  • Tabletop / exhibit — an exhibit table or demo space at the venue.
  • In-kind support — venue, AV, printing, or other in-kind contributions.
Packages coming soon. Sponsorship tiers, pricing, and benefits will be published once approved by the committee. In the meantime, reach out and we'll follow up directly.
