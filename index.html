IEEE Conference #72378 · November 21, 2026

2026 IEEE Smart City USA Conference

Urban innovation with measurable community impact

Speaker proposal submissions are open. The submission deadline is September 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM Central Time (CT). The conference takes place Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Collin College – Wylie Campus Conference Center and online.

A premier one-day conference bringing government officials, industry leaders, and technical experts together to exchange practical smart-city use cases, development insights, and innovative urban solutions. This is a hybrid conference held in Wylie, Texas, and online. The in-person venue is Collin College – Wylie Campus Conference Center, 391 Country Club Rd, Wylie, TX 75098.

Technical program

Technical tracks connecting technology and urban outcomes

The program uses the following six technical tracks:

  1. Smart City Infrastructure, Public Services & Community Resilience
  2. Sustainable Cities, Climate Resilience & Smart Energy
  3. Smart Mobility, Transportation & Connected Systems
  4. Data Spaces, AI, Digital Twins & Smart City Platforms
  5. IoT, Sensors, Connectivity, Cybersecurity & Privacy
  6. Semiconductors, AI Hardware, Edge Intelligence & Data Centers

Government officials, industry leaders, technical experts, researchers, students, and practitioners are invited to exchange useful technical experience and practical solutions.

Sponsored by IEEE Smart Cities. This event is sponsored by the IEEE Smart Cities Ambassadors Program.
