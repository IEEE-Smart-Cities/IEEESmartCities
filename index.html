2026 IEEE Smart City USA Conference

Urban innovation with measurable community impact

Call for Speakers is now open. Submit your proposal by September 30, 2026. The conference takes place Saturday, November 21, 2026, in Wylie, Texas.

A premier one-day conference bringing government officials, industry leaders, and technical experts together to exchange practical smart-city use cases, development insights, and innovative urban solutions. The event is listed by IEEE as a hybrid conference based in Wylie, Texas.

Technical program

Six tracks connecting technology and urban outcomes

The program covers Smart Mobility & Transportation, IoT & Edge Computing, AI & Big Data in Urban Environments, Sustainable Energy, Smart Urban Infrastructure, and Smart Government.
