IEEE Smart Cities Dallas

From Chips to Cities: Building Smart, Resilient, and Connected Communities

Call for Speakers is now open. Submit your proposal by September 30, 2026. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas will be held in person on Saturday, November 21, 2026.

Connecting semiconductors, AI, data centers, infrastructure, energy, mobility, and public services for the future of smart cities. A regional IEEE Smart Cities technical event for industry professionals, academia, government, students, Women in Engineering (WIE), and Young Professionals (YP) — gathering in person in Plano, Texas.

Why this event

Cross-disciplinary, from silicon to city services

Smart cities need work that spans infrastructure, chips, AI, energy, mobility, governance, cybersecurity, and community impact. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas brings those communities together in person.

The program is intended for engineers, researchers, public-sector leaders, educators, students, and practitioners who want to exchange useful technical experience.

Technically Sponsored by IEEE Smart Cities Community.
