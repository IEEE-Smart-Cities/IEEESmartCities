From Chips to Cities: Building Smart, Resilient, and Connected Communities

Call for Speakers is now open. Submit your proposal by September 30, 2026. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas will be held as a live hybrid event on Saturday, November 21, 2026.

Connecting semiconductors, AI, data centers, infrastructure, energy, mobility, and public services for the future of smart cities. A regional IEEE Smart Cities technical event for industry professionals, academia, government, students, Women in Engineering (WIE), and Young Professionals (YP) — attend in person in Plano, Texas or join online.
