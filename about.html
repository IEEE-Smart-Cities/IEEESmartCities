About the Event

Building smart, resilient, and connected communities

Smart cities require cross-disciplinary work — across infrastructure, chips, AI, energy, mobility, governance, cybersecurity, and community impact. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas is a regional technical event bringing these communities together in person on Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Collin College – Plano Campus Conference Center in Plano, Texas.

Event details

The conference takes place Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Collin College – Plano Campus Conference Center in Plano, Texas. It is an in-person, one-day regional event. Check-in, parking, and room information will be published with the final agenda.

Who should attend

The event is for industry professionals, researchers, public-sector leaders, educators, students, Women in Engineering, Young Professionals, and community organizations working on smart-city technology and services.

How the program is selected

The program will combine invited contributions with selected session proposals. Independent reviewers will assess proposals against the published criteria, and each proposal should explain its connection to at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal.

Speaker requirements and deadlines are listed on the Call for Speakers page.
