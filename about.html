About the 2026 IEEE Smart City USA Conference

The 2026 IEEE Smart City USA Conference is a premier one-day conference uniting government officials, industry leaders, and technical experts to collaborate on smart city topics, including innovative urban solutions, development insights, and practical use cases.

The conference organizes its program across six technical tracks. Proposals are evaluated for relevance, practical value, speaker experience, originality, clarity, and connection to at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG).

  1. Smart City Infrastructure, Public Services & Community Resilience
  2. Sustainable Cities, Climate Resilience & Smart Energy
  3. Smart Mobility, Transportation & Connected Systems
  4. Data Spaces, AI, Digital Twins & Smart City Platforms
  5. IoT, Sensors, Connectivity, Cybersecurity & Privacy
  6. Semiconductors, AI Hardware, Edge Intelligence & Data Centers
