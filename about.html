About the Event

Building smart, resilient, and connected communities

Smart cities require cross-disciplinary work — across infrastructure, chips, AI, energy, mobility, governance, cybersecurity, and community impact. IEEE Smart Cities Dallas is a regional technical event bringing these communities together on Saturday, November 21, 2026, as a hybrid event: in person at Collin College – Plano Campus Conference Center in Plano, Texas, and online.

📅

Date

Saturday, November 21, 2026. All published deadlines use Central Time (CT).

📍

Venue

Collin College – Plano Campus Conference Center, Plano, TX, USA.

🌐

Format

A hybrid one-day program of talks, panels, workshops, and demonstrations. Speakers and attendees can participate in person or online.

Speaker-focused this cycle

This year's event is a speaker-only program — we are not running a paper submission or peer-review process, and there are no proceedings. The program includes invited talks, speaker proposals, panels, case studies, workshops, demonstrations, and dedicated student, Young Professionals (YP), and Women in Engineering (WIE) sessions.

Who it's for

Industry professionals, researchers, public-sector and government leaders, academia, students, Women in Engineering (WIE), Young Professionals (YP), and community stakeholders working on any layer of the smart-city stack — from semiconductors to public services.

UN Sustainable Development Goals. Speakers are asked to show how their presentation supports at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG). This connects technical work to broader community and global impact, and every topic track is mapped to the goals it most often touches.
Hybrid Participation

Taking part in person or online

IEEE Smart Cities Dallas accepts both in-person and virtual speakers. Hybrid joining instructions for attendees will be published with the final agenda on November 13, 2026.

In person · Plano, TX

At the venue

Collin College – Plano Campus Conference Center. Check-in details, parking, and the room schedule are published with the final agenda.

  • Speaker check-in ahead of your session block.
  • Final slides due Friday, November 6, 2026, 5:00 PM CT and loaded on the venue system in advance.
  • Bring your own adapters if presenting from your own laptop.
  • Special AV needs should be raised as early as possible after acceptance.
Online · Virtual speakers

Presenting remotely

Virtual talks are a full part of the program, not an afterthought — remote speakers are reviewed on the same rubric as in-person speakers.

  • Stable internet connection, working webcam and microphone, and a quiet presentation environment.
  • Required technology check between November 9 and November 12, 2026.
  • Final slides due November 6, 2026; backup slides may be requested to support event-day continuity.
  • Backup contact details — phone or alternate email — in case of connection trouble on the day.

Full speaker expectations and the complete milestone schedule are on the Call for Speakers page.

Planning status. The date and venue above reflect the current conference record. Sponsorship packages, registration pricing, and the final agenda are still being finalized by the committee and will be published as they are approved.
